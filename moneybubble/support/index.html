Common questions

  • Where is my data? Only on your iPhone or iPad. There’s no account, so nothing syncs between devices.
  • How do I restore Premium on a new device? Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • How do I cancel Premium? In the App go to Settings → Manage subscription, or open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.
  • How do I start over? Settings → Reset all data in the App deletes everything you’ve entered.
