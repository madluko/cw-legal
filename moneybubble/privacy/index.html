Privacy Policy — Moneybubble

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Bundle ID: com.bufferuk.app · Contact: luke@pureformcreations.com

Summary

  • Everything you enter (income, bills, balances, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The App has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. It makes no network requests to us.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • We do not collect, see, sell or share your personal or financial data.

Data stored on your device

To work out your cash buffer, runway and safe-to-spend figures, the App stores on your device:

  • Your current balance, income entries, bills and dated one-off payments
  • Settings such as your buffer floor (in weeks) and tax pot percentage
  • First-run and alert preference flags

This data never leaves your device through the App. It may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud or computer backups) under Apple’s terms. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all data in the App, or by deleting the App.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules local alerts on your device (for example when your buffer is forecast to drop below your floor). These are created on the device; nothing is sent to a server.

Subscriptions

Moneybubble Premium is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your card details or Apple Account information. The App only checks with Apple on your device whether you have an active subscription. Apple’s privacy policy applies to purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings. Terms of use: Apple Standard EULA.

Not financial advice

Moneybubble is a budgeting tool. Its figures are estimates based on what you enter and are not financial or tax advice.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above.
